A private Algarve experience created for two

Your time on the coast, without a shared tour group

Boat4Two hosts only one couple or two guests at a time. Your skipper and guide welcome you in Ferragudo, then the tour follows the nearby Algarve coastline past beaches, cliffs and coastal landmarks.

This daytime private boat tour is available at the current fixed times shown in the Boat4Two booking calendar. Availability is always checked through the main booking system.

What makes Boat4Two different

Private by design

Just two guests

The experience is exclusive to two passengers, accompanied by a guide and skipper. You do not join a larger sightseeing group.

There is time to enjoy the Algarve coast, have a drink, take photos and use the activities on board when conditions are suitable.

What you can expect

A coastal route from Ferragudo

The published route starts in Ferragudo and can include Castelo de Ferragudo, Ponta do Altar Lighthouse, Praia da Rocha, Praia do Vau, Praia do Alemão and João de Arens Beach before returning to Ferragudo.

Sea and weather conditions always influence what is comfortable and safe on the day. Swimming and the equipment on board are enjoyed when conditions are suitable.

Frequently asked questions

Is the boat tour completely private?

Yes. Every Boat4Two tour is private and reserved for just two guests at a time.

The tour starts and finishes in Ferragudo. Meeting details are provided after booking.

What is included in the daytime tour?

Drinks, snacks, kayak, SUP, snorkeling gear, photos, videos and a Polaroid photo album are included on board.

The kayak, SUP and snorkeling gear are available on board, and water activities can be enjoyed when conditions are suitable.

If Boat4Two cancels due to weather or safety reasons, you may reschedule or receive a full refund.
