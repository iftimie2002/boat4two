Why privacy matters

Only the two of you are booked

Boat4Two experiences are exclusive to two passengers, accompanied by a guide and skipper. You are not proposing in front of a shared group of tourists, and the booking form already lets you mention a proposal or surprise as the occasion.

Contact Boat4Two before the tour so the crew understands your plan. They can discuss realistic timing and available arrangements without promising weather, an exact sunset or a particular point on the coast.

A practical proposal plan

How to prepare without spoiling the surprise

1. Contact Boat4Two early

Use the existing contact or WhatsApp option and explain that the booking is a proposal. Keep personal requests realistic and agree only what the team confirms.

2. Discuss the moment

Share whether you prefer to propose earlier in the trip or around sunset. The skipper must keep safety and real sea conditions as the priority.

3. Keep details discreet

Use a private message channel and include the occasion in your booking notes. Avoid placing important surprise details where your partner may see them.

Photos and memories

Use the inclusions already available

The published Boat4Two tours include photos, videos and a Polaroid photo album. If capturing the proposal is important, discuss it beforehand so the crew knows your intention. This does not guarantee a particular photograph or filming angle.

Weather flexibility

Plan for the sea, not against it

Weather and sea conditions can change, and Boat4Two may postpone or cancel for safety. If Boat4Two cancels, the current policy allows rescheduling or a full refund. Keep another proposal plan in mind if the date is especially important.

Frequently asked questions

Planning a proposal

Will other tourists be on the boat?

No. A Boat4Two tour is reserved for two passengers, together with the guide and skipper.

Can the crew help coordinate the proposal moment?

Contact Boat4Two beforehand to discuss your idea, timing and any arrangements the team can genuinely provide. Safety and conditions on the day remain the priority.

Are photos included?

The published tours include photos, videos and a Polaroid photo album. Discuss the proposal in advance if capturing the moment is important.

Is sunset guaranteed?

No. Sunset light and visibility depend on weather and sea conditions. Choose the sunset tour for the planned timing, but keep expectations flexible.

Where does the tour begin?

Boat4Two tours start and finish in Ferragudo. Meeting details are sent after booking.
