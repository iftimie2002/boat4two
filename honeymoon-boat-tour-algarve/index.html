Privacy without a group itinerary

Your own time on the water

Boat4Two reserves each experience for one couple or two guests, with a guide and skipper. There is no shared tourist group, making it easier to relax and enjoy the coast at a quieter pace.

Both the daytime and sunset experiences depart from Ferragudo and last 3.5 hours. The current booking calendar confirms which dates and times are genuinely available.

Frequently asked questions

Honeymoon tour questions

Is the boat private for honeymooners?

Yes. Boat4Two tours are reserved for two passengers only, accompanied by a guide and skipper.

Which tour is more romantic?

The sunset tour adds golden-hour and sunset timing, while the daytime tour offers a bright and relaxed coastal experience. Choose the atmosphere that suits you.

Are photos included?

Photos, videos and a Polaroid photo album are included in the published tour information.

Where does the honeymoon experience start?

Both current tours start and finish in Ferragudo. Meeting details are provided after booking.
