Clear departure information

The meeting point is in Ferragudo, not Portimão

Ferragudo sits on the eastern side of the Arade River, facing Portimão and Praia da Rocha. Boat4Two uses Ferragudo as the start and finish point for its private tours. Exact meeting details are provided after booking.

If you are staying in Portimão, Praia da Rocha, Carvoeiro or elsewhere in the western Algarve, plan your journey to Ferragudo before the scheduled departure. Transportation to Ferragudo is not included.

The geographical context

Ferragudo, Portimão and the nearby coastline

Ferragudo

The fishing village is Boat4Two's real departure and return point. Castelo de Ferragudo appears early in the published route.

Portimão and Praia da Rocha

The route crosses the coastal context shared by Ferragudo and Portimão, with Praia da Rocha among the listed landmarks.

Further west along the coast

The published route can continue past Praia do Vau, Praia do Alemão and João de Arens Beach before returning to Ferragudo.

Private format

One couple, not a shared tourist boat

Each tour is exclusive to two passengers, with a guide and skipper. This is different from a shared sightseeing departure where unrelated groups follow the same fixed group experience.

Boat4Two is suitable for couples who prefer privacy, a quieter pace and space for a celebration, honeymoon or proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Ferragudo and Portimão

Does the boat leave from Portimão?

No. Boat4Two tours start and finish in Ferragudo, on the opposite side of the river from Portimão.

Is transportation from Portimão included?

No. Transportation to Ferragudo is not included.

How many tourists are on the tour?

The tour is reserved for two passengers only, accompanied by a guide and skipper.
