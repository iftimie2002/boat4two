FAQ

What to know before you book.

Is the tour private?

Yes. Every tour is private and reserved for just two guests at a time.

Where does the tour start?

The tour starts in Ferragudo.

How do we reach the boat?

We will give you the meeting details after booking.

What is included?

Drinks, snacks, kayak, SUP, snorkeling gear, photos, and a Polaroid photo album are included on board. The sunset tour also includes sunset views from the sea.

Can we swim or do activities in the water?

Yes. On board you will have access to the kayak, SUP board, and snorkeling gear during the tour.

Is the tour suitable for wheelchair users?

No, it is not suitable for wheelchair users.

Can we buy extra drinks on board?

Yes. Extra beverages can also be purchased on board.

What if weather conditions are not safe?

If we cancel the tour due to weather or safety reasons, the full amount will be refunded.
