A romantic sunset Algarve experience

Golden hour without a crowded tour boat

The Boat4Two sunset tour is reserved for only two guests. Your guide and skipper welcome you in Ferragudo for a private route along the coast, with time to enjoy drinks and snacks as daylight changes over the water.

It is designed for couples, proposals, anniversaries, birthdays, honeymoons or a quiet evening together. Sunset and sea conditions vary naturally, so the crew keeps the experience comfortable and safe rather than promising an exact view or location.

Ferragudo and the coast

What to expect

The published route begins and ends in Ferragudo and follows the nearby coastline past beaches and landmarks. The sunset route includes a sunset moment near the Ponta do Altar area when conditions allow.

Water activities are available on board and can be used when sea and weather conditions are suitable. The booking calendar on the homepage shows the current available dates and times.

Frequently asked questions

Sunset tour questions

Is the sunset tour shared with other tourists?

No. The sunset tour is completely private and reserved for only two guests.

How long is the Boat4Two sunset tour?

The current sunset tour lasts 3.5 hours and departs from Ferragudo.

What is included in the sunset tour?

Drinks, snacks, kayak, SUP, snorkeling gear, photos, videos and a Polaroid photo album are included, together with sunset views from the sea.

Can I organise a marriage proposal on the sunset tour?

The private two-guest format is suitable for proposals. Contact Boat4Two before booking to discuss the timing and any available arrangements.

What happens if the weather is not safe?

If Boat4Two cancels for weather or safety reasons, you may reschedule or receive a full refund.
